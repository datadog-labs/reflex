Three policies, one workload
All lanes receive identical offered requests. Placement is deterministic FIFO. Nodes move through Off → Starting → Ready → Draining → Off. Starting and draining nodes count toward the budget and node-seconds.
Toto receives 256 one-second samples of offered jobs, CPU-seconds and GiB-seconds, and forecasts 120 seconds. The initial history is synthetic prehistory from the selected pattern. Actual future arrivals, task completion times and the scenario name are never given to Toto or Jev.
Forecasting runs at most every 10 simulated seconds and every 5 wall-clock seconds, with one request in flight, an 8-second deadline and 60-call run limit. Forecasts expire after 30 simulated seconds. Fast playback can outpace inference. Missing or stale forecasts trigger an explicitly labeled reactive fallback.
Jev recommends hold, start one, start two or drain one. Reflex rechecks freshness, lifecycle, cooldown, budget and resource invariants. A starting node offers no usable capacity. Pointwise p10–p90 bounds are not a calibrated confidence guarantee.
Replay re-applies recorded traffic, forecast availability and decisions without calling Toto or Jev. Export includes the full journal. Jev cost is a cumulative estimate for this server session; unreported usage is not included. Toto monetary pricing is unavailable.
